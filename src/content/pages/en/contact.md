We welcome inquiries, feedback, and contributions from readers, cultural historians, researchers, and partners around the world. Whether you have identified an error, wish to share insights about your community's heritage dress, or have questions about our content, we are here to help.

## Direct email {#email}

For all correspondence, please email our editorial desk at:

**[contact@traditionalclothinghub.com](mailto:contact@traditionalclothinghub.com)**

We review every incoming message and typically respond within two to three business days.

## Editorial feedback and corrections {#corrections}

Accurate cultural documentation is central to our mission. If you find information that requires updating, clarification, or correction:
- Please specify the exact page title or URL.
- Describe the proposed change or missing historical detail.
- Whenever possible, include reputable reference sources such as academic books, museum collection entries, or scholarly papers to help our editorial team verify the information swiftly.

## Image rights and attribution {#image-rights}

We place high importance on respecting copyright, creative licenses, and cultural stewardship. The imagery displayed across Traditional Clothing Hub is sourced from public domain collections, museum open-access initiatives, and open-license repositories, all catalogued on our [image sources](/sources/) page.

If you are a photographer, artist, or representative of a cultural institution and believe that an image has been misattributed, requires updated licensing details, or should be removed, please contact us immediately with the image file name or URL. We will address your request promptly.

## Partnerships, advertising and collaborations {#partnerships}

We collaborate with educational initiatives, museums, cultural preservation societies, and relevant organizations dedicated to textile preservation and cultural heritage. For partnership inquiries, educational republishing requests, or advertising inquiries, please email us with details of your organization and proposal.

## Community suggestions {#suggestions}

Is there a country, ethnic group, or traditional garment you would love to see featured next? Send us your suggestions! While our editorial queue is planned in advance, community interest directly informs which regional traditions we prioritize.
