We are happy to hear from you, whether you have found a mistake, want to suggest a country or garment, or have a question about one of our images.

## Email {#email}

Write to us at **[contact@traditionalclothinghub.com](mailto:contact@traditionalclothinghub.com)**. We aim to reply within a few working days.

## Corrections and suggestions {#corrections}

Please tell us which page you mean and what should be changed. If you can, include a source such as a book, a museum record or an article. It helps us check the correction quickly.

## Image rights {#images}

All images on this site are either in the public domain or used under the licence shown on our [image sources](/sources/) page. If you are the author of an image and believe it is credited incorrectly, or you would like it removed, please email us with the image's file name and we will act promptly.
